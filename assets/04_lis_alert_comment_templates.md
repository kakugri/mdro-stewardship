# LIS Alert And Comment Template Bank

Title: Neutral Laboratory Comment Examples For Rapid MDRO-Related Results

Author: Marious Akugri

Version: 1.0 public reference release

Date: 2026-08-25

## Summary

This template bank provides neutral, synthetic examples of LIS alert and result-comment wording for rapid MDRO-related laboratory findings. The examples are designed to help laboratories communicate result status, pending confirmation, pending AST, infection-prevention awareness, stewardship awareness, and final-result follow-up without directing clinical treatment.

These examples are not approved institutional comments. They must not be inserted into a live LIS unless reviewed and approved through local laboratory, medical, infection prevention, stewardship, quality, compliance, and LIS governance.

This template bank is for local adaptation discussion and review. It does not represent a live LIS build, employer-approved comment bank, or clinical validation record.

## General Comment Formula

Use a three-part structure:

```text
[Result status]. [Laboratory limitation or pending follow-up]. [Local-policy routing or correlation statement].
```

Example:

```text
Rapid molecular testing detected [organism/resistance marker]. Culture and/or susceptibility testing may be pending. Correlate with final laboratory results and institutional notification protocols.
```

## Wording Controls

Use language that:

- identifies the laboratory finding;
- states whether the result is preliminary, final, corrected, or pending confirmation;
- notes whether culture, organism identification, AST, or confirmatory testing may still be pending;
- routes awareness according to local policy; and
- avoids patient-specific treatment direction.

Avoid language that:

- selects an antimicrobial or antifungal agent;
- states a drug should be started, stopped, changed, or avoided;
- directs isolation or transfer decisions;
- claims final confirmation when confirmation is pending;
- bypasses local institutional policy; or
- claims adoption by any institution.

## Locally Adaptable Example Terms

Use the bracketed example terms below to show where locally approved laboratory language would be inserted during a governed adaptation process:

| Example Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[organism]` | Organism detected or identified. |
| `[resistance marker]` | Molecular resistance marker or resistance-related finding. |
| `[specimen type]` | General specimen category. Do not include patient identifiers. |
| `[method]` | Locally approved or validated method, if appropriate for comment use. |
| `[local policy]` | Institution-specific protocol or notification pathway. |
| `[receiving team]` | Locally approved team or route, such as provider, infection prevention, or stewardship queue. |

## Template 1: Preliminary Rapid Molecular Detection

Use when a rapid molecular result is available before full culture or susceptibility follow-up.

```text
Rapid molecular testing detected [organism] from [specimen type]. Culture and/or susceptibility testing may be pending. Correlate with final laboratory results and institutional notification protocols.
```

Short version:

```text
Preliminary rapid molecular result: [organism] detected. Final culture and/or susceptibility results may be pending.
```

Scope note:

This comment communicates laboratory status only. It does not direct clinical treatment.

## Template 2: Resistance Marker Detected, AST Pending

Use when a molecular resistance marker is detected but phenotypic AST is not final.

```text
Rapid molecular testing detected [resistance marker]. Phenotypic susceptibility testing may be pending. Correlate with final AST and institutional notification protocols.
```

Alternative:

```text
[resistance marker] detected by rapid molecular testing. Final organism identification and/or susceptibility testing may be pending.
```

Scope note:

Do not imply that final phenotypic susceptibility has been completed if AST is pending.

## Template 3: Organism Identification Pending Confirmation

Use when organism identification may require confirmation or further characterization.

```text
Preliminary organism identification suggests [organism or organism group]. Additional confirmation or characterization may be pending according to laboratory protocol.
```

Alternative:

```text
Organism identification is preliminary and may require confirmatory testing. Interpret with final laboratory report and local notification protocol.
```

Scope note:

Do not label an organism as confirmed if local confirmation criteria have not been met.

## Template 4: C. auris Concern Pending Confirmation

Use when C. auris is suspected or confirmation is pending.

```text
Preliminary laboratory finding raises concern for Candida auris. Confirmatory identification and institutional notification steps may be pending according to local protocol.
```

Alternative:

```text
Candida auris confirmation status: pending. Follow local laboratory and infection prevention notification procedures.
```

Scope note:

This comment should not be used to imply confirmed C. auris unless confirmation criteria are satisfied.

## Template 5: Confirmed C. auris Laboratory Result

Use only when local confirmation and reporting criteria are satisfied.

```text
Candida auris identified by [method]. Follow institutional infection prevention, communication, and reporting protocols as applicable.
```

Alternative:

```text
Confirmed Candida auris laboratory result. Communicate status according to local infection prevention and transfer communication procedures.
```

Scope note:

The comment communicates laboratory status. Infection prevention actions and patient-care decisions remain governed by local policy.

## Template 6: AST Pending

Use when an organism result is available but susceptibility testing is not final.

```text
[organism] identified. Antimicrobial susceptibility testing is pending. Correlate with final AST report when available.
```

Alternative:

```text
Organism identification available; susceptibility testing not yet final.
```

Scope note:

Do not infer susceptibility or resistance beyond the reported result status.

## Template 7: AST Finalized

Use when susceptibility testing is final and local result release is complete.

```text
Final antimicrobial susceptibility testing is available for [organism]. Interpret according to the final laboratory report and institutional clinical protocols.
```

Alternative:

```text
Final AST result posted for [organism]. Review final laboratory report for susceptibility interpretation.
```

Scope note:

The comment points to the final laboratory report. It does not choose treatment.

## Template 8: Corrected Result Follow-Up

Use when a corrected report changes a prior organism, resistance-marker, AST, or status communication.

```text
Corrected laboratory result issued for [organism/result category]. Prior preliminary or final communication may require review according to local corrected-result notification policy.
```

Alternative:

```text
Corrected report: [result category] updated. Follow institutional corrected-result communication procedure.
```

Scope note:

Corrected result comments should follow local policy and be documented through approved systems.

## Template 9: Discrepant Rapid And Culture/AST Result

Use when a rapid finding and later culture/AST workup do not align.

```text
Rapid result and follow-up culture and/or susceptibility findings require technical review. Interpret according to final corrected laboratory report and local documentation protocol.
```

Alternative:

```text
Result discrepancy identified between preliminary rapid testing and follow-up laboratory findings. Technical review and final reporting are pending.
```

Scope note:

Do not suppress discrepancies without technical review under local policy.

## Template 10: Interfacility Transfer Communication Reminder

Use only where local policy supports transfer communication reminders.

```text
MDRO or Candida auris status may require communication during patient transfer or discharge according to institutional policy.
```

Alternative:

```text
If transfer or discharge occurs, communicate applicable MDRO/Candida auris status according to local infection prevention and transfer communication procedures.
```

Scope note:

This comment does not determine transfer eligibility, bed placement, isolation orders, or patient management.

## Template 11: Infection Prevention Awareness

Use where local policy includes infection prevention routing for specified findings.

```text
This laboratory result may meet institutional criteria for infection prevention awareness. Route according to local notification procedure.
```

Alternative:

```text
Infection prevention notification may be indicated under local policy for this result category.
```

Scope note:

The laboratory routes awareness; infection prevention determines local action.

## Template 12: Antimicrobial Stewardship Awareness

Use where local policy includes stewardship routing for specified findings.

```text
This result category may be reviewed by antimicrobial stewardship according to institutional process. Correlate with final organism identification and AST status.
```

Alternative:

```text
Stewardship awareness route may apply under local policy. Final culture and/or AST status should be reviewed when available.
```

Scope note:

The laboratory does not recommend medication selection.

## Comment Review Checklist

Before a comment is adapted, ask:

- Does it state result status clearly?
- Does it avoid therapy recommendations?
- Does it avoid isolation or transfer orders?
- Does it distinguish preliminary, final, corrected, and pending results?
- Does it avoid overstating rapid molecular findings?
- Does it route awareness only under local policy?
- Has LIS/informatics reviewed the wording and display behavior?
- Has laboratory/medical leadership approved the comment?
