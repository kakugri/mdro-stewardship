# Result-Status Taxonomy

Title: Common Vocabulary For Rapid MDRO Laboratory Communication

Author: Marious Akugri

Version: 1.0 public reference release

Date: 2026-08-25

## Purpose

This taxonomy defines common result-status language used across the public artifact suite. The goal is to make laboratory communication more consistent when rapid MDRO-related information is preliminary, pending confirmation, final, corrected, or discrepant.

This document is a reference vocabulary. It does not override local reporting rules, laboratory director approval, medical director approval, public-health reporting requirements, or institutional policy.

## Taxonomy Table

| Result Status | Working Meaning | Laboratory Communication Risk | Suggested Communication Posture | Scope Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Preliminary rapid molecular result | A rapid molecular assay has detected an organism, marker, or target before full culture or susceptibility context is complete. | Treating the rapid result as final. | State that the result is from rapid molecular testing and note pending culture, AST, or final report status where applicable. | Do not select therapy. |
| Organism identification pending confirmation | Initial organism identification may require additional confirmation, referral, or technical review. | Overstating uncertain organism identity. | Use cautious wording and follow local confirmation and reporting pathways. | Especially important for unusual yeast identification and suspected C. auris. |
| Resistance marker detected | A resistance gene or marker is detected by rapid method. | Equating marker detection with complete phenotypic susceptibility interpretation. | Identify the detected marker and note pending or final AST status. | Local validation and reporting rules control final wording. |
| AST pending | Susceptibility testing has not been finalized. | Closing the communication loop before final susceptibility information is available. | Communicate rapid or preliminary information with clear AST pending status. | Create follow-up trigger where local process requires it. |
| AST final | Susceptibility testing is finalized and available for reporting. | Failure to reconcile earlier preliminary comments with final AST. | Review whether earlier comments, alerts, or notifications require update. | Still does not imply treatment direction by the laboratory artifact. |
| Corrected result | A corrected report changes earlier result information. | Earlier communication may remain in circulation without follow-up. | Document correction, route follow-up under local policy, and update comments if appropriate. | Correction handling must follow institutional procedures. |
| Discrepant rapid/culture result | Rapid result and culture, AST, or organism identification do not fully align. | Conflicting communication or unsupported certainty. | Escalate for technical review and avoid definitive wording until resolved under local process. | Preserve local quality and technical review requirements. |
| Suspected C. auris | Findings suggest possible C. auris but confirmation is pending or not yet complete. | Premature confirmed-status language or missed infection-prevention awareness route. | Use suspicion/confirmation-pending wording and route awareness according to local policy. | Does not establish a diagnosis or containment order. |
| Confirmed C. auris | Local or reference method confirms C. auris under approved reporting criteria. | Incomplete communication of status during care transitions. | Follow local infection prevention, public-health, and transfer-communication procedures. | Communication wording must match local approved language. |
| Negative rapid test with culture pending | Rapid testing does not detect the target, but culture or other final workup remains pending. | Treating a negative rapid test as a final exclusion. | State rapid-test limitation and continue final-result follow-up where applicable. | Avoid closing follow-up prematurely. |
| Notification documented | A required or selected communication pathway has been completed and recorded. | Missing audit trail. | Record route, time, role/recipient category, and follow-up flag under local policy. | Do not create duplicate documentation outside approved systems. |
| Follow-up pending | A final, corrected, confirmatory, or AST-related step remains unresolved. | Loss of continuity across shifts or sections. | Maintain a local follow-up queue or flag if approved. | The format depends on the local LIS and quality system. |

## Status-Aware Comment Pattern

Neutral comments should answer four questions without directing clinical care:

1. What was detected or changed?
2. What method or status applies?
3. What remains pending, if anything?
4. What local follow-up route applies, if any?

Example pattern:

```text
[Method/status] detected [organism, marker, or result change]. [Culture, AST, confirmation, or final report] is [pending/final/corrected] as applicable. Follow institutional notification and follow-up procedures.
```
