Laboratory-facing rapid MDRO diagnostic stewardship workflow.

A public, non-confidential dry-run reference for result-status review, reflex and confirmatory follow-up, LIS alert/comment drafting, notification documentation, TAT tracking, and staff training around rapid MDRO-relevant laboratory findings.

Scope Boundary

  • Laboratory communication and diagnostic stewardship support.
  • Synthetic examples and public guidance only.
  • No patient data, employer SOPs, or institutional adoption claims.
  • No live LIS configuration or clinical assay validation claim.
  • No treatment, prescribing, isolation, transfer, or reporting direction.
01 Status-aware

Preliminary, final, corrected, pending AST, and confirmation-dependent paths are kept separate.

02 Dry-run safe

Examples use adaptable terms and synthetic scenarios rather than patient or employer information.

03 Locally governed

Any real use remains subject to laboratory director, medical, quality, LIS, and institutional review.

Review Path

How to evaluate the package quickly

The reviewer path is designed to make technical feedback specific. Reviewers can test the workflow against synthetic scenarios, compare output language to the comment bank, and return a focused memo, checklist, or signed letter.

1 Confirm the boundary

Read the scope and non-scope statements before evaluating any sample workflow.

2 Run dry cases

3 Check wording

Compare generated output with neutral LIS comment examples and local-policy caveats.

4 Return focused feedback

Use the reviewer checklist to identify strengths, limits, and useful revisions.

      LIS Comment Bank

      Neutral wording examples for local review

      These examples state laboratory status and pending follow-up while avoiding treatment direction. Local governance controls any real LIS wording.

      Preliminary rapid result

      Rapid molecular testing detected [organism] from [specimen type]. Culture and/or susceptibility testing may be pending. Correlate with final laboratory results and institutional notification protocols.

      Resistance marker, AST pending

      Rapid molecular testing detected [resistance marker]. Phenotypic susceptibility testing may be pending. Correlate with final AST and institutional notification protocols.

      C. auris pending

      Preliminary laboratory finding raises concern for Candida auris. Confirmatory identification and institutional notification steps may be pending according to local protocol.

      C. auris confirmed

      Candida auris identified by [method]. Follow institutional infection prevention, communication, and reporting protocols as applicable.

      Corrected result

      Corrected laboratory result issued for [organism/result category]. Prior preliminary or final communication may require review according to local corrected-result notification policy.

      Discrepant result

      Rapid result and follow-up culture and/or susceptibility findings require technical review. Interpret according to final corrected laboratory report and local documentation protocol.

      Synthetic Dry-Run Cases

      Load a case into the workflow builder

      The scenarios test whether the workflow handles preliminary findings, pending confirmation, final AST follow-up, C. auris status, and corrected-result communication.

      S1. Rapid marker with AST pending

      A blood culture rapid molecular panel detects Organism X and Resistance Marker Y. Culture and AST are pending.

      S2. Organism ID with AST pending

      An organism is identified, but antimicrobial susceptibility testing has not finalized.

      S3. Suspected C. auris

      Yeast identification suggests possible C. auris, but confirmatory testing or reference review is pending.

      S4. Confirmed C. auris

      A locally approved or reference method confirms C. auris and local routing may apply.

      S5. Corrected organism ID

      A final organism identification is corrected after an earlier preliminary communication.

      S6. Negative rapid, culture pending

      A rapid target is not detected, but culture or other final laboratory workup remains pending.

      S7. Discrepant rapid/culture result

      A rapid result detects a marker, but later culture or AST context does not clearly align.

      Reviewer Checklist

      Technical review questions

      Reviewers should evaluate technical usefulness, laboratory realism, wording discipline, and safety boundaries. The package is not presented as an approved institutional protocol, clinical validation record, or evidence of adoption.

      Suitable reviewers may include laboratory directors, microbiology supervisors, molecular diagnostics professionals, infection preventionists, antimicrobial stewardship pharmacists, infectious disease physicians, or laboratory quality professionals.

      Core Checks

      • Understandable to laboratory professionals.
      • Realistic for high-complexity laboratory review.
      • Clear about preliminary, final, corrected, pending, and confirmation-dependent results.
      • Neutral in LIS comment wording.
      • Adaptable by more than one laboratory under local governance.
      • Specific about limits, revisions, and useful next steps.
